1. How we’ve addressed prior feedback
Sam and Rajiv suggested using Snakemake as origanizational tools for our pipeline. We thought this was a useful suggestion and considered adopting it, but decided to continue developing our existing Python workflow because most of the established scripts and pipelines were already made and we had already invested time in connecting the analysis scripts and HPC workflow as is. In response to Rajiv’s feedback about interpreting our figures, we noticably see regions within the p24 protein that have higher entropy than others - likely due to the importance of those strucutres in HIV efficacy. We've extended our visualizations to connect sequence variability with structural regions and solvent accessibility to further assess this and help with interpretability in the structural domain. We (with the help of HopGPT) have created a flowchart in the docs/ to explain the progression of our methods and make the scripts, input files, and outputs easier to follow for its current stage

2. New progress since the last submission
Our main progress has been developing additional code to integrate structural information with our entropy analysis and generate the following figures:
•	Overall entropy plots with structural annotations: These show entropy across p24 amino acid positions, with a second panel showing reference solvent accessibility
•	Variant-specific entropy plots: These compare entropy patterns among variant groups alongside a shared reference solvent accessibility panel although specific variants are difficult to tell apart
•	Entropy versus relative solvent accessibility scatterplots: These display variability against accessibility, with points colored by structural region
We created both major and minor region versions of the structural plots. The major-region versions distinguish NTD, IDR, and CTD, while the minor-region versions show more detailed features, including individual helices, providing different levels of detail for examining where variability occurs. We also added code to map alignment columns to HXB2 p24 amino acid positions and combine variant entropy values with structural annotations and reference solvent accessibility. The combined data are saved as csv files for further analysis. Our new flowchart documents the workflow from sequence extraction, gap removal, and short-sequence filtering through alignment, frequency and entropy calculations, reference mapping, and visualization. In the overall entropy plots, many positions have entropy near zero, with scattered peaks of higher variability. This provides a starting point for identifying variable positions and evaluating whether their patterns differ among structural regions or relate to solvent accessibility.
